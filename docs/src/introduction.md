# Introduction

This project contains the control software and documentation for the University of Florida Machine Intelligence Lab's (MIL) autonomous unmanned arial vehicle (UAV), InvestiGator.

The InvestiGator project is developed for application in the biannual RobotX competition held by RoboNation. InvestiGator's goal as an autonomous vehicle is to cooperate with MIL's other robots, SubjuGator and NaviGator, to perform autonomous challenges presented at RobotX.

The InvestiGator project is built and maintained by the team of students involved with MIL under the guidance of MIL Director Dr. Eric Schwartz. This project, and MIL in general, gives students a chance to learn skills and apply themselves to hands-on, real-world applications in an environment that encourages experimentation, leadership, and growth. The initial author of this text would like to give a warm thank you to Dr. Schwartz and everyone in the lab who puts in the hard work to contribute to everything we do in the lab. It has been a worthwhile experience.
